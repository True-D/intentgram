Intently Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Intently is a Chrome extension that reorganizes the Instagram home feed you are already viewing, so you can browse it by topic, place, time and account. This policy explains what data it handles and where that data goes.

The short version

  • Everything Intently stores stays on your computer, inside your browser.
  • We don't run any servers, and we never receive, sell or share your data.
  • The only outside service it contacts is a free map lookup, to find which city or country a tagged place is in. That lookup sends map coordinates and place names only.

What Intently reads

When you browse instagram.com, Intently reads the feed data the Instagram page has already loaded in your browser:

  • captions, hashtags and image descriptions
  • account names
  • tagged places and when posts were published
  • image links

It also notes which feed posts appeared on your screen, to show how many of the posts you saw it captured. It makes no extra requests to Instagram, and it never posts, likes, follows or changes anything on your account. It does not read your messages, password or login details.

What Intently stores, and where

All of this is kept in the extension's storage on your computer:

  • the captured posts and a small copy of each post's image
  • the categories you choose for accounts
  • your ★ saved posts
  • your settings
  • the places it has already looked up

Posts are deleted automatically after the period you choose in Manage (30 days by default). ★ saved posts are kept until you remove them.

Data sent outside your computer

  • Instagram's image servers (cdninstagram.com, fbcdn.net): Intently downloads each captured post's image once, so it can still be shown after Instagram's image links expire. This is the same kind of request your browser makes when you view the post.
  • Photon by komoot (photon.komoot.io), a free OpenStreetMap place service: to let you search posts by area, Intently sends the map coordinates of places tagged in posts, and the place names you type in the Place filter. It never sends post captions, images, account names or anything about you.
  • Chrome's on-device AI (optional): if you use the AI sorting button, post text is processed by the model built into Chrome, on your computer. Nothing is sent to an AI company.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information, analytics or tracking data.
  • We don't sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We don't use data for anything other than organizing your feed.
  • We don't use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Your control

  • In Manage, you can delete all captured posts or turn off saving images.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored.

Not affiliated with Instagram

Intently is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.
